Lock photo: editing it in the main window

Clicking Lock Photo… switches the window into photo mode, with no separate window. The deck shows the photo exactly as its 14 screens will, and the parts the frames hide show faintly between them, so you can place the photo deliberately. In this example the eyes land in the gap between the two key rows, which is the problem you've had with the fixed crop. Dragging the photo down a little puts them on a key. With "Show on the deck" on, the real N4 shows it too while you edit.

  • Why not a separate window: it would be a second layer, which you didn't want for presets, and it would only reach the deck after you click Save. Here every drag shows on the deck at once.
  • The lock photo belongs to the N4 itself, not to a preset. With the N3 selected, the Lock Photo… button isn't shown, since the N3 has no lock photo.
